I'm designing a chip for Jane Street's ASIC competition. The goal is a chip that can be reprogrammed after it is made to speak protocols like UART, SPI and I2C. Designs go through the Tiny Tapeout flow on IHP's 130 nm process, and entries are due January 18, 2027.
